One Down Golf

Track every bet, settle every round.

One Down Golf is a golf scorecard and bet-tracking app for golfers who play money games with their group. Enter scores hole by hole, and the app calculates who owes whom across Nassau, Skins, Wolf, and more. No money is transacted through the app — all settlement happens between players in person.

Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email hello@onedown.golf and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Enter your email and we'll send you a one-time code — no password needed.
  • How do I add friends to a round? Share your round's join code, or add them from your friends or club list.
  • Does the app handle money? No. It only calculates what's owed; you settle up in person.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete Account, or email us.
